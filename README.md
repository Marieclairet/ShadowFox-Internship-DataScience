### Task 2 – Delhi Air Quality Index (AQI) Analysis
- Goal: Investigate major pollutants contributing to Delhi’s poor air quality and identify trends.
- Dataset: Public Delhi AQI CSV (daily readings).
- Approach:
  1. Load and preprocess data (pandas).
  2. Check missing values and generate descriptive statistics.
  3. Visualize pollutant trends over time (matplotlib).
- Key Findings:  
  - PM2.5 & PM10 levels are dangerously high, posing severe health risks.  
  - CO spikes suggest traffic or industrial events.  
  - Recommendations include stricter emission regulations and long-term green initiatives.  
  - Full analysis in [Air Quality Index Analysis (PDF)](TASK%202/AIR%20QUALITY%20INDEX%20ANALYSIS%20.pdf)
